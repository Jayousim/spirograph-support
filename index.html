Quick tips

  • Drag in circles to roll the gear and draw.
  • Flick and release to keep it spinning; touch and hold still to stop it.
  • Use the controls at the bottom to choose a stage, pen hole, ink color, and spin speed.
  • Turn on Auto to spin hands-free. Choose Clear to start a fresh drawing.

Your stage progress and preferences are saved on your device.
